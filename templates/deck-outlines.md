# Deck outlines

Every deck follows the project's `deck` slot for format and theme. Numbers come only from `sizing.md`. Wording matches evidence: Inferred and Assumed claims are hedged, and external evidence is "evidence suggests", never a promise for this customer base.

## Pitch deck

1. Title: the idea in one line
2. The problem, with Verified numbers where we have them
3. Who is affected: segment size and profile
4. Options (2–3), each with pros and cons
5. Why it could work: business case, psychology, examples from other companies (Sourced)
6. Recommendation and what we'd test first
7. Risks
8. Assumptions to confirm (business-execution assumptions first)
9. Ask: what we need decided in this meeting
10. Sources

## Results deck (experiment round n)

1. Title: <idea>, round n results
2. What changed since round n-1 (and the feedback that drove it)
3. Impact sizing: before vs after
4. Who moved: segments that gained, lost or kept the same outcome
5. Surprises and checks we ran
6. Changes from round n-1 numbers (from round 2 on)
7. Assumptions to confirm
8. Ask: adjust (what), or ship
9. Sources

## Final deck

1. Title: <idea>, shipped
2. What we changed and why
3. Final impact numbers
4. How to read the new results (for the business team)
5. What to monitor
6. Assumptions to confirm
7. Sources and links to notes
